## Platform

## Stack

Astro (static output), content kept in structured content collections so a git-based admin (Keystatic) can be added later without a rebuild. ## Users

IM Projekt does not treat these groups differently; every commission is evaluated on its own merits regardless of the client's size.

- **Developers and investors:** large companies with above-average budgets looking for design contractors, from partial work (structural engineering, parts of large complexes, technical solutions) to complete design of apartment buildings, hospitals and other facilities.
- **Families and individuals:** people who need to live somewhere, usually financed by a mortgage. They want either full care from design to supervision of construction, or a paid consultation for a single step of a build or renovation.
- **Public administration and municipalities:** public tenders, subsidy-funded projects, civic amenities in towns and villages.
- **Architects and design offices:** IM Projekt prepares the higher documentation stages from their designs when they lack the capacity, experience or time.
- **Job candidates (urgent):** young building designers, including students at technical faculties, for employment and contractor (IČ) roles.

## Product Purpose

IM Projekt spol. s r. o. is a design and engineering office in Mladá Boleslav, operating since 1992 (groundwork 1990). It designs residential, civic and industrial buildings, handles engineering (permits and negotiation with authorities), author supervision, budgets and tender documentation, and offers marketing and branding. The website must give the office a new, modern face, confirm its professionalism to every audience, bring more commissions, and recruit new staff.

## Positioning

Claim: **Partner pro vaši ideu.** A long-standing (30+ years) office that takes a client's idea through every stage, from study to documentation, permits and supervision, with an engineer's precision. Planned divisions will extend this to construction, real estate and marketing under the IM Projekt name, covering the whole path from drawing to sale.

## Operating Context

Construction design in the Czech Republic: documentation stages under decree 131/2024 Sb. (studie, DUR/DSP/DPS/DPZ, dokumentace skutečného provedení), public procurement under Act 134/2016 Sb. and decree 169/2016 Sb., itemized budgets, bills of quantities, author supervision. Drawings use the Romans CAD font; company documents use Arial. Visitors compare offices by their references and clients.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages from the current site: home, O nás, Služby (with four audience sections and three services), Reference (filterable, ~64 projects, detail pages), Aktuality (articles), Kariéra (open positions + unsolicited CV), Kontakty, cookie policy, GDPR.
- Contact form with file attachment.
- Future: four divisions (projekční kancelář, stavební společnost, realitní kancelář, marketing), possibly colour-coded. Structure must allow adding them without a rebuild; they are not designed yet.

- Language: Czech only.

## Brand Commitments

- Logo (orange mark + grey lowercase "improjekt") is untouchable in shape. A gentle upgrade (clean flat versions) is allowed. The client likes the glassy effect of the current header logo and wants glass on buttons and elsewhere.
- Colours derive from the logo: orange mark, grey wordmark. Shades may vary.
- Lowercase as a "young" trait carries through communication, but visitors are always addressed formally (vykání), including students.
- Tone: technical, precise, simple, technical elegance; "smart detail" rather than flourishes.
- Existing website copy stays. The client is proud of it; only typos and small stylistic fixes are allowed. No new claims.

## Product Principles

1. Precision is the proof: every detail on the site must be correct, because the office sells accuracy.
2. References carry the argument; show real projects with their real scope.
3. One partner for every scale: a family house and a hospital get the same seriousness.
4. Recruitment is a first-class path, not a footer link.
5. Built to grow into divisions without redesign.
